## About

**Identity tags:** Social Development & Protection Professional · Leadership & People Development Practitioner · Inspirational Speaker · Spiritual Mentor

**Lead statement:** I empower people and strengthen communities, through professional practice and through faith.

### Professional journey

Over more than twenty years, I have worked across policy advocacy, education programming, social protection, gender and disability inclusion, and child protection in Uganda and across East, West and Central Africa. My experience includes FAWE Uganda, World Vision Uganda, the Ministry of Education and Sports, and independent consultancy. I hold an MA in Social Sector Planning and Management from Makerere University.

### How I work

My strengths are training, mentorship, leadership development and capacity strengthening. I build effective teams, nurture talent, and help organisations achieve meaningful and sustainable results. Whether I am facilitating a workshop, mentoring an individual, inspiring an audience or supporting organisational development, my aim is to equip people and strengthen purpose.

### Faith & ministry

For the past three years, alongside my professional work, I have been engaged in spiritual development and ministry through Bible-based teaching, prayer, fellowship and spiritual mentorship. Through shared Bible study, prayer gatherings and reflective conversations, I help people deepen their understanding of God's Word, grow in faith and apply biblical principles to everyday life.

**Closing line:** My approach brings together professional competence, people-centred leadership and faith-informed values, with a commitment to lasting transformation in individuals, organisations and communities.

## Services

**Section heading:** How I can help
**Intro:** Six areas of advisory, training and technical support, tailored to your organisation, your context and your funders' requirements.

### 1. Safeguarding & PSEAH

**Who it's for:** NGOs, schools, refugee-response organisations, donor-funded projects.
**What I offer:**

- Independent, survivor-centred investigations into sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment (PSEAH)
- Safeguarding policies, codes of conduct, referral pathways and reporting protocols
- GBV and sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA) prevention training and awareness materials
  **Proof point:** Member of an independent PSEAH investigators team for UNHCR-funded organisations, investigating in schools in refugee settlements across Uganda and Kenya.

### 2. Gender, Disability & Social Inclusion

**Who it's for:** NGOs, government programmes, donors, education institutions.
**What I offer:**

- Gender and disability inclusion assessments and audits
- Inclusion policies, strategies and practical toolkits
- Mainstreaming inclusion into programme design and M&E frameworks
  **Proof point:** Led World Vision Uganda's organisation-wide Gender Assessment, which informed its Gender and Disability Inclusion Policy.

### 3. Education Research, Programme Review & Policy Advocacy

**Who it's for:** Donors, international NGOs, education programmes, civil society coalitions.
**What I offer:**

- Programme reviews and evaluations with clear, actionable recommendations
- Research and policy briefs that turn field evidence into advocacy
- Advocacy strategies and engagement with ministries, Parliament and donor missions
  **Proof point:** Reviewed the Student Referral Pathways Programme in Uganda and Kenya for WUSC, and contributed to review of Uganda's Re-entry to School Policy and the National Strategy for Girls' Education.

### 4. Training & Capacity Building

**Who it's for:** Schools, teacher training institutions, civil society organisations, national institutions.
**What I offer:**

- Gender-responsive pedagogy (GRP) training as a certified GRP Toolkit Trainer
- Life skills and psychosocial well-being training
- Practical workshops on proposal development, financial accountability and donor regulations
  **Proof point:** Trained educators from 10 West and Central African countries on GRP centres of excellence, and supported 23 schools in Ntoroko District (2024).

### 5. Grants Management & Resource Mobilisation

**Who it's for:** Local and national NGOs, research institutions, organisations preparing for donor funding.
**What I offer:**

- Grants management systems, due diligence and sub-grantee oversight
- Audit preparedness and donor compliance support
- Proposal development and funding strategy
  **Proof point:** Mobilised over UGX 21 billion from Irish Aid, the African Development Bank, MasterCard Foundation and Dubai Cares, and secured 90% of FAWE Uganda's 2013–2017 strategic plan budget.

### 6. Leadership, Mentorship & Speaking

**Who it's for:** Organisations, leadership teams, youth and women's programmes, conferences and events.
**What I offer:**

- Leadership and people development programmes
- Structured mentorship for emerging leaders, adolescent girls and young women
- Inspirational talks and keynotes on purpose, leadership and service
  **Proof point:** Built and led a team of 14 at FAWE Uganda, and mentor adolescent girls and young women through Girls 4 Girls Uganda and FAWE Uganda.

## Approach

**Section heading:** How I work
**Intro:** Every engagement is practical, evidence-based and tailored to your context.

1. **Listen and diagnose:** I start by understanding your goals, your context and your funder or regulatory requirements.
2. **Design a fit-for-purpose approach:** A clear scope, timeline and deliverables, with no generic templates.
3. **Deliver with rigour:** Careful fieldwork, survivor-centred and inclusive practice, and regular communication.
4. **Leave capacity behind:** Practical tools, trained people and systems that your team can keep using.

**Principles:** Evidence-led · Inclusive and survivor-centred · People-first · Accountable to funders and communities
